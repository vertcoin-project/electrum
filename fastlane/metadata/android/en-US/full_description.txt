Electrum-VTC is a libre self-custodial Vertcoin wallet, based on Electrum.

Features:
• Safe: Your private keys are encrypted and never leave your device.
• Open-source: MIT-licensed free/libre open-source software, with reproducible builds.
• Forgiving: Your wallet can be recovered from a secret phrase.
• Instant On: Electrum-VTC uses servers that index the Vertcoin blockchain, making it fast.
• No Lock-In: You can export your private keys and use them in other Vertcoin wallets, Vertcoin Core included.
• Your Choice of Server: Use any of the public Electrum-VTC servers, or your own.
• Proof Checking: Electrum-VTC verifies all the transactions in your history using SPV, and the block headers it downloads by their Verthash proof-of-work and Kimoto Gravity Well difficulty.
• Cold Storage: Keep your private keys offline and go online with a watching-only wallet.

Lightning is not supported.

Electrum-VTC is a fork of Electrum (https://electrum.org) by Electrum Technologies GmbH, for Vertcoin. It is not endorsed by the Electrum developers.
